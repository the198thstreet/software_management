  • Combine and display Google and Kakao calendars, events, and tasks for the date selected by the user.

Google and Kakao data is processed only after the user explicitly grants the requested permissions on each service's login and consent screen. Users may use the extension without connecting a service they do not want to use and may disconnect a service in Settings.
