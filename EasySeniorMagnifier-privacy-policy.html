This Privacy Policy explains how the Easy Senior Magnifier Android app accesses, uses, retains, and deletes user data.

The app does not provide account registration, login, payment, advertising, or internet communication with a developer-operated server. The developer does not maintain user accounts or a user database for this app.

Camera frames, frozen frames, and recognized text are processed on the device to provide app features. The app does not save photos, recognition results, or synthesized audio as files.

1. Data Processed by the App

The app processes the following information to provide magnification and text read-aloud features. The developer does not collect or receive this information.

Data type How it is processed Purpose
Camera frames After camera permission is granted, frames from the rear camera are shown as a live preview. Frames are processed in device memory and are not saved as photos or videos or transmitted to a developer-operated server. To magnify small text and objects, focus the view, and illuminate the subject with the device light.
Frozen frame image When the user freezes the screen, one current camera frame is temporarily retained in device memory for display. It is not saved as a file in the gallery or app storage. To let the user examine a magnified view without holding the device still.
Image used for text recognition and recognized text Only after the user selects text reading, the current or frozen frame is processed on the device by the bundled Google ML Kit Korean text-recognition model. Recognized text is displayed as returned and is not automatically saved or transmitted to the developer. To display camera-visible text in large type and make it available for read-aloud.
Text selected for read-aloud When the user selects read aloud, recognized text is passed to the device's configured Android TextToSpeech engine. The app does not save synthesized audio to a file. To let the user listen to recognized text.

2. Camera Permission and User Choice

  • The app requests camera permission to provide live magnification, the device light, screen freeze, and text recognition.
  • If camera permission is denied, magnifier features are unavailable, but the app shows a clear permission screen instead of closing or displaying a blank screen.
  • Users can change or revoke camera permission at any time in Android settings.
  • The app does not request microphone, location, contacts, phone, SMS, storage, photo, or video access permission.
  • The app itself does not declare internet or network-state permission. However, the device's configured text-to-speech engine may use a network connection under its own permissions and settings.

3. Data Transmission and External Services

The app does not upload or sell camera frames, frozen images, or recognized text to a developer-operated server, and does not share them for advertising. The following device functions or external services may process information for their respective roles.

  • Google ML Kit Korean text recognition: The Korean recognition model is bundled with the app and processes images and text on the device only after the user selects text reading. The app does not transmit this data to an external server for text recognition.
  • Android camera service: The app uses Android system and device-manufacturer camera functions to provide preview, zoom, focus, and the device light.
  • Android text-to-speech service: Read aloud uses the device's configured TextToSpeech engine. Depending on the engine and selected voice, recognized text may be processed on the device or sent to the engine provider for network-based synthesis. Subsequent processing is governed by that provider's privacy policy and the user's settings.
  • GitHub Pages: The provider hosting this Privacy Policy may process ordinary web request information such as IP address, access time, browser, and user agent while serving the page.
  • Legal requirements: Information may be provided when required by applicable law, court order, or a valid government request. However, the developer does not maintain an app user database.

Information processed by Google ML Kit, Android system services, text-to-speech engines, and GitHub Pages may be governed by each provider's policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub General Privacy Statement

4. On-Device Retention and Deletion

  • Live camera frames are processed in device memory while displayed and are not saved as photos or videos by the app.
  • A frozen frame is no longer retained after the user resumes the preview, leaves the screen, or closes the app.
  • Recognized text is processed in device memory while the result screen is shown and is not stored in a database or file by the app.
  • The app does not save audio generated for read-aloud as a file.
  • App-specific data is configured to be excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • Retention and deletion of data processed by a text-to-speech engine are governed by that provider's policy and the user's settings.
  • The app does not create user accounts, so there is no separate account-deletion process.

5. Security

The app processes only the minimum data needed for its features. Camera frames and recognized text are processed in device memory and are not stored. The app itself does not use internet permission or a developer-operated server.

Security of text passed to a read-aloud engine depends on that engine, the operating system, the device manufacturer, and the user's settings.

6. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The app does not use advertising SDKs, personalized advertising, user-behavior analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, or developer-operated tracking services. It does not use an advertising identifier to track users or build user profiles.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is a general-purpose tool for users who have difficulty seeing small text or objects and is not primarily directed to children. The app does not collect user accounts or personal information, and the developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated due to changes in app features, device services, applicable laws, or Google Play policies. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date or last-updated date on this page.
