1. 앱에서 처리하는 데이터

앱은 돋보기, 글자 읽기 및 광고 기능을 제공하기 위해 아래 정보를 처리합니다. 개발자는 카메라 영상, 멈춘 화면 또는 인식된 글자를 자체 서버로 수집하거나 전송받지 않습니다.

  • 사용자는 Android 설정에서 언제든지 카메라 권한을 변경하거나 철회할 수 있습니다.
  • 앱은 마이크, 위치, 연락처, 전화, 문자, 저장공간 또는 사진·동영상 접근 권한을 요청하지 않습니다.

3. 데이터 전송 및 외부 서비스

앱은 카메라 영상, 멈춘 화면 또는 인식된 글자를 개발자 서버로 업로드하거나 판매하지 않으며 Google 광고 서비스로 전송하지 않습니다. 다음 기기 기능 또는 외부 서비스가 각자의 역할에 따라 정보를 처리할 수 있습니다.

  • Google ML Kit 한국어 글자 인식: 한국어 인식 모델이 앱에 포함되어 있으며, 사용자가 글자 읽기를 선택했을 때 이미지와 글자를 기기 안에서 처리합니다. 앱은 글자 인식을 위해 해당 데이터를 외부 서버로 전송하지 않습니다.
  • Android 카메라 서비스: 카메라 미리보기, 확대, 초점 및 손전등을 제공하기 위해 Android 시스템과 기기 제조사의 카메라 기능을 사용합니다.
  • Android 음성합성 서비스: 읽어주기 기능은 기기에 설정된 기본 TextToSpeech 엔진을 사용합니다. 엔진과 선택된 음성에 따라 인식된 글자가 기기 안에서 처리되거나 네트워크 기반 음성 합성을 위해 엔진 제공자에게 전달될 수 있습니다. 이후 처리는 해당 제공자의 개인정보 처리방침과 사용자 설정을 따릅니다.
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK 및 UMP SDK: 광고 제공, 동의 및 개인정보 선택 관리, 광고 측정과 부정 사용 방지를 위해 위에 설명한 광고·기기·진단 정보와 동의 정보를 처리할 수 있습니다. 카메라 영상, 멈춘 화면 및 인식된 글자는 Google 광고 서비스로 전송되지 않습니다. Google Mobile Ads SDK가 전송하는 데이터는 전송 중 TLS로 암호화됩니다.

6. 광고, 분석 및 사용자 추적

앱은 화면 하단에 Google Mobile Ads SDK의 배너 광고를 표시할 수 있습니다. 카메라 사용, 화면 멈춤 또는 글자 읽기 기능의 이용 조건으로 광고 클릭이나 시청을 요구하지 않습니다.

앱은 실행 시 Google UMP SDK를 통해 동의 정보를 갱신하고 필요한 지역에서는 광고 동의 화면을 제공합니다. 해당 지역에서는 앱 종료 확인 화면의 “광고 개인정보 설정”을 통해 선택을 다시 확인하거나 변경할 수 있습니다. 필요한 동의를 받지 못했거나 광고 요청이 허용되지 않은 경우 앱은 광고를 요청하지 않습니다.

앱은 개발자 자체 사용자 행동 분석 또는 추적 서비스를 사용하지 않습니다. 사용자는 Android 또는 Google 설정에서 광고 ID를 재설정·삭제하거나 광고 맞춤설정을 변경할 수 있으며, 광고의 맞춤 여부와 제공되는 선택지는 지역, Google 설정 및 관련 법령에 따라 달라질 수 있습니다.

Easy Senior Magnifier Privacy Policy

App name: Easy Senior Magnifier (돋보기 - 어르신을 위한 쉬운 돋보기)

Developer/Operator: The198thStreet

Privacy contact: czerny2k@naver.com

This Privacy Policy explains how the Easy Senior Magnifier Android app accesses, uses, retains, and deletes user data.

The app does not provide account registration, login, or payment and does not operate a developer-controlled user server or database. A banner ad delivered through Google Mobile Ads may appear at the bottom of the app.

Camera frames, frozen frames, and recognized text are processed on the device to provide app features. The app does not save photos, recognition results, or synthesized audio as files.

1. Data Processed by the App

The app processes the following information to provide magnification, text read-aloud, and advertising. The developer does not collect camera frames, frozen images, or recognized text through its own server.

Data type How it is processed Purpose
Camera frames After camera permission is granted, frames from the rear camera are shown as a live preview. Frames are processed in device memory and are not saved as photos or videos or transmitted to a developer-operated server. To magnify small text and objects, focus the view, and illuminate the subject with the device light.
Frozen frame image When the user freezes the screen, one current camera frame is temporarily retained in device memory for display. It is not saved as a file in the gallery or app storage. To let the user examine a magnified view without holding the device still.

Text selected for read-aloud When the user selects read aloud, recognized text is passed to the device's configured Android TextToSpeech engine. The app does not save synthesized audio to a file. To let the user listen to recognized text.

Advertising privacy choices Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK may check and process advertising consent status and privacy choices in applicable regions. Provide consent and privacy controls required by applicable law and determine whether ads may be requested.

2. Camera Permission and User Choice

  • The app requests camera permission to provide live magnification, the device light, screen freeze, and text recognition.
  • If camera permission is denied, magnifier features are unavailable, but the app shows a clear permission screen instead of closing or displaying a blank screen.
  • Users can change or revoke camera permission at any time in Android settings.
  • The app does not request microphone, location, contacts, phone, SMS, storage, photo, or video access permission.
  • The app uses internet and network-state permissions to deliver Google advertising and advertising privacy controls and to check network connectivity.
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK may use the Android advertising ID and related identifiers for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • The device's configured text-to-speech engine may use a network connection under its own permissions and settings.

3. Data Transmission and External Services

The app does not upload or sell camera frames, frozen images, or recognized text to a developer-operated server and does not transmit them to Google advertising services. The following device functions or external services may process information for their respective roles.

  • Google ML Kit Korean text recognition: The Korean recognition model is bundled with the app and processes images and text on the device only after the user selects text reading. The app does not transmit this data to an external server for text recognition.
  • Android camera service: The app uses Android system and device-manufacturer camera functions to provide preview, zoom, focus, and the device light.
  • Android text-to-speech service: Read aloud uses the device's configured TextToSpeech engine. Depending on the engine and selected voice, recognized text may be processed on the device or sent to the engine provider for network-based synthesis. Subsequent processing is governed by that provider's privacy policy and the user's settings.
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK and UMP SDK: May process the advertising, device, diagnostic, and consent information described above for advertising, consent and privacy-choice management, measurement, and fraud prevention. Camera frames, frozen images, and recognized text are not transmitted to Google advertising services. Data transmitted by Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • GitHub Pages: The provider hosting this Privacy Policy may process ordinary web request information such as IP address, access time, browser, and user agent while serving the page.
  • Legal requirements: Information may be provided when required by applicable law, court order, or a valid government request. However, the developer does not maintain an app user database.

Information processed by Google ML Kit, Google advertising services, Android system services, text-to-speech engines, and GitHub Pages may be governed by each provider's policy.

4. On-Device Retention and Deletion

  • Live camera frames are processed in device memory while displayed and are not saved as photos or videos by the app.
  • A frozen frame is no longer retained after the user resumes the preview, leaves the screen, or closes the app.
  • Recognized text is processed in device memory while the result screen is shown and is not stored in a database or file by the app.
  • The app does not save audio generated for read-aloud as a file.
  • App-specific data is configured to be excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • Retention and deletion of data processed by a text-to-speech engine are governed by that provider's policy and the user's settings.
  • Retention and deletion of data processed by Google for advertising and consent features are governed by Google's policies and the user's Google and Android advertising settings.
  • The app does not create user accounts, so there is no separate account-deletion process.

5. Security

The app processes only the minimum data needed for its features. Camera frames and recognized text are processed in device memory, are not stored, and are not transmitted to a developer-operated server. Advertising-related network data is transmitted in encrypted form through Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Security of text passed to a read-aloud engine depends on that engine, the operating system, the device manufacturer, and the user's settings.

6. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The app may display a banner ad from Google Mobile Ads SDK at the bottom of the screen. The app does not require an ad click or ad view as a condition of using the camera, screen-freeze, or text-reading features.

On launch, the app uses Google UMP SDK to update consent information and, where required, show an advertising consent form. In applicable regions, users can review or change their choices through “Ad privacy settings” in the app's exit confirmation dialog. The app does not request ads if required consent has not been obtained or ad requests are not permitted.

The app does not use developer-operated behavioral analytics or tracking services. Users can reset or delete their advertising ID or change ad personalization settings in Android or Google settings. Ad personalization and available choices may vary by region, Google settings, and applicable law.

7. Children's Privacy
